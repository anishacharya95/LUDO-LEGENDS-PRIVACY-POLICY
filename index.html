Privacy Policy

Ludo Legends · Effective date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ludo Legends (the “Game”), published by the Ludo Legends developer (“we”, “us”, or “our”), handles information when you use the Game.

1. Information handled by the Game

The Game may process gameplay information and technical information needed to run, maintain, and troubleshoot the app. The exact information depends on the features enabled in the version you use. This policy specifically describes the Google Mobile Ads integration identified for the current project. Any additional services in a release build (for example, analytics, crash reporting, sign-in, multiplayer, cloud saves, or purchases) must also be disclosed here before that build is published.

2. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The Game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertising, including app-open ads and interstitial ads. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or receive information from the app and your device to deliver, measure, and protect advertising services. Depending on your device settings, consent choices, and location, this may include:

  • Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers;
  • Device model, operating system, language, and app information;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Ad impressions, interactions, and related diagnostic or fraud-prevention information.

Google may use this information to provide, personalize, measure, and improve ads, and to detect invalid activity. Ad personalization and consent options may vary by region and device. You can review or change available advertising and privacy controls in your Android or Google settings.

Google’s handling of information is governed by Google’s own policies. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. How information is used

Information handled by the Game or its advertising provider may be used to operate and maintain the Game, show and measure advertisements, prevent fraud and abuse, diagnose technical problems, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

4. Sharing

Google Mobile Ads and its service providers may process the advertising-related information described above. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect the Game, its users, or the public. Any additional third-party SDKs or online services included in a release build must be identified in this section.

5. Data retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Google and its partners determine retention periods for information they collect under their own policies. We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Children’s privacy

The Game’s intended age group and advertising configuration must comply with the laws and app-store requirements that apply in each region where it is distributed. Google’s advertising services may apply age-related restrictions and protections. If the Game is directed to children or is likely to be used by children, its release configuration and this policy must be reviewed to ensure that only permitted data collection and advertising are used.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal information, or to object to particular processing. You can also use the privacy and advertising controls available on your device and in Google services. Requests concerning information collected by Google should be directed to Google through its privacy tools.

8. Third-party services and links

The Game’s advertising functionality is provided by Google Mobile Ads. Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review those policies.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when the Game, its services, or applicable legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this page with a revised effective date. Your continued use of the Game after an update means you have had an opportunity to review the revised policy.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through the Ludo Legends Privacy Policy repository. Please do not post sensitive personal information publicly in an issue.
